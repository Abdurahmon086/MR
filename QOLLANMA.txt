 1. TIZIM HAQIDA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Bu tizim dermatologik kasalliklarni tashxis qilish, bemor
ma'lumotlarini saqlash va AI yordamida teri rasmlarini tahlil
qilish uchun mo'ljallangan to'liq axborot tizimi.

TEXNOLOGIYALAR:
  Backend  : Python 3.14 + FastAPI + PostgreSQL 16
  Frontend : Next.js 14 + TypeScript + Ant Design 5
  AI Model : BiT-ResNet50 (HuggingFace) + Dermoskopik tahlil
  Auth     : JWT (access 60 daqiqa + refresh 30 kun)
  PDF      : ReportLab (o'zbek/rus/ingliz)

 4.3 TASHXISLAR BO'LIMI
────────────────────────────────────
URL: http://localhost:3000/diagnoses

  • Filterlash: Og'irlik darajasi, Holati bo'yicha
  • Ustunlar: Kod, Sana, Tashxis matni, ICD-10, Og'irlik, Holat
  • "Ko'rish" tugmasi → Tashxis tafsiloti

YANGI TASHXIS QO'SHISH:
  URL: /diagnoses/new  (yoki bemor profilidan)
  Majburiy: Bemor, Qabul sanasi, Shikoyat, Tashxis matni
  Ixtiyoriy: ICD-10 kod, Og'irlik darajasi, Anamnez,
             Davolash rejasi, Keyingi qabul sanasi
  Tashxis kodi avtomatik yaratiladi: DX-2026-NNNNN

TASHXIS TAFSILOTI:
  Chapda:
    → Barcha tashxis ma'lumotlari jadvalda
    → Shikoyat, anamnez, davolash rejasi matni
    → Teri rasmlari galereyasi (yuklangan bo'lsa)
  O'ngda:
    → AI tahlil natijalari (rasm yuklangan bo'lsa)
    → Ishonch darajasi grafigi (7 sinf)
    → Doktor tasdiqlash tugmalari

  Tugmalar:
    → "PDF Hisobot" → Bemor xulosasi PDF yuklanadi
    → "Tahrirlash" → Modal oynada o'zgartirish

OG'IRLIK DARAJASI RANGLARI:
  Yashil  = Yengil (mild)
  To'q sariq = O'rtacha (moderate)
  Qizil   = Og'ir (severe)
  Binafsha = Juda og'ir (critical)

────────────────────────────────────
 4.4 AI TAHLIL BO'LIMI
────────────────────────────────────
URL: http://localhost:3000/ai-analysis

ISHLATISH TARTIBI:
  [1] Bemorni tanlang (qidirish orqali)
  [2] Teri rasmini yuklang (JPEG, PNG, BMP — max 20MB)
      → Rasmni tortib tashlash yoki "Yuklash" tugmasi
  [3] "Tahlil qilish" tugmasini bosing
  [4] Natijani kuting (1-5 soniya)

NATIJA KO'RINISHI:
  Yuqorida: Xavf darajasi tegi (Past/O'rtacha/Xavfli/Juda xavfli)
  Katta bloK: Aniqlangan kasallik nomi + ICD-10 kod + tavsif
  Ishonch darajasi: 0-100% progress bar
  Barcha 7 sinf: Har biri uchun ehtimollik foizi

  Kasallik sinflari:
    mel   = Melanoma (KRITIK — darhol shifokorga!)
    bcc   = Bazal hujayra karsinomasi (XAVFLI)
    akiec = Aktinik keratoz (XAVFLI)
    vasc  = Qon-tomir shikastlanishi (O'RTACHA)
    bkl   = Xavfsiz keratoz (PAST)
    df    = Dermatofibroma (PAST)
    nv    = Melanositar nevus/xollar (PAST)

DOKTOR BAHOLASHI:
  Natijani ko'rib "Roziman" yoki "Rozimas" bosing
  Izoh yozing (ixtiyoriy)
  → Tasdiqlangandan keyin yashil banner ko'rinadi
  → "O'zgartirish" tugmasi bilan qayta baholash mumkin

MUHIM ESLATMA:
  AI natijasi yordam vositasi — yakuniy tashxis
  faqat mutaxassis shifokor tomonidan qo'yilishi kerak!

 7. AI MODEL HAQIDA
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MODEL: Fu-chiang/bit-50-skin-lesions (BiT ResNet-50)
       + Dermoskopik rang/tekstura tahlili

DATASET: HAM10000 (10,015 ta dermoskopik rasm, 7 sinf)

  Sinf  | Nomi                      | ICD-10 | Xavf
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  mel   | Melanoma                  | C43    | KRITIK
  bcc   | Bazal hujayra karsinomasi | C44    | YUQORI
  akiec | Aktinik keratoz           | L57.0  | YUQORI
  vasc  | Qon-tomir shikastlanishi  | D18    | O'RTACHA
  bkl   | Xavfsiz keratoz           | L82    | PAST
  df    | Dermatofibroma            | D23    | PAST
  nv    | Melanositar nevus (xollar)| D22    | PAST

MODEL ISHLASH TARTIBI:
  1. Yuklangan rasm preprocessing (224x224 pixel)
  2. BiT ResNet-50 → 3 asosiy sinf ehtimolligi
  3. Dermoskopik rang tahlili → 4 qo'shimcha sinf
  4. Birlashtirish va normalizatsiya → 7 sinf natija
  5. Ishonch darajasi, xavf darajasi, ICD-10 qaytariladi

ANIQLIK:
  Haqiqiy dermoskopik rasmlarda yaxshi natija beradi.
  Oddiy fotografiyalar uchun aniqlik pasayishi mumkin.
  Yakuniy tashxis HAMISHA shifokor tomonidan tasdiqlanishi shart!
